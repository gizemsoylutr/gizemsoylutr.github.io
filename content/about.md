Hey — I'm Gi.

I design intelligent enterprise systems on SAP BTP, where cloud-native architecture meets Generative AI and agentic automation.

At **NTT DATA Business Solutions Denmark**, I design CAP and RAP-based solutions enhanced with SAP AI Core, SAP Joule, and SAP Build Agents — enabling AI-driven decision support, intelligent document processing, and autonomous workflow execution.
My work focuses on transforming traditional enterprise applications into adaptive, AI-augmented systems.

Beyond project work, I share practical tutorials on my YouTube channel <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@btpstack" target="_blank"><strong>BTP Stack</strong></a>. Code-first content that reflects how SAP BTP is actually used in production.
